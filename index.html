BanVoyage Press Room

Honest family travel reviews, room by room

What makes BanVoyage different

Most family travel content is written from press photos and brochures. BanVoyage only reviews trips the Banvilles took themselves, with their own photos and video. The room tours answer the question parents actually search for before booking: which bed does each person sleep in, and does it work for a family of four?

Not a travel agency

BanVoyage is an independent review site. It doesn’t sell trips or book travel. Some pages carry affiliate links, which are disclosed on the page and in the site’s affiliate disclosure. A commission never changes a review or a rating.

The people behind it

Founders

Brooke Banville

Co-founder, filmmaker and lead writer

Brooke films the room tours and writes most of BanVoyage, starting from her own notes on each trip. She is a Certified Public Accountant who spent 17 years in finance at Ernst & Young, Qlik and SAP, and she reviews a trip the way an auditor would: what it really cost, what the fine print says, and whether the room matches the listing. She studied accounting at Temple University’s Fox School of Business.

BanVoyage (banvoyage.com) is a family travel review site founded by Brooke and Laurence Banville of Delaware County, Pennsylvania. The Banvilles review only places they have stayed themselves, with their own photos, video and the prices they paid, and their room tours show exactly where each family member sleeps. BanVoyage covers cruises, Disney parks, all-inclusive resorts, winter trips and weekends close to home, and rates each stay with the BanVoyage Rating, from 0 to 5. Follow along on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest and Facebook.
